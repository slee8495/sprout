## Full description

Sourced from the sl-studio.dev/roun marketing copy — kept in sync with that page's voice.

```
Save the moments you'll want to hand back to them, someday.

A private, shared journal for your family — not every photo, just the moments you'll want to remember: the date, and what you were thinking when it happened.

A camera roll can hold the photo. It can't hold the memory.

WHY NOT JUST KEEP PHOTOS ON YOUR PHONE?
Ten years of that and you'll have tens of thousands of photos — most of them near-duplicates, with no way to find the one that mattered or remember what you were thinking when you took it. Roun only holds the moments you chose to keep: dated, with your own words attached, and searchable by keyword — so it becomes one story you can hand back someday, not a camera roll you'd never finish scrolling.

THE NAME
Roun has its roots in Old Norse, where it means mysterious wisdom, or a secret — the same origin as the English word "rune." It's also a word in Khmer, the language of Cambodia, meaning garden.

So that's what we wanted this app to become: a beautifully tended garden, a precious family secret-book recording your kids and pets as they grow.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
📷 Photos & voice memos — attach a photo or a quick voice memo to any entry
🎉 Milestones — track firsts, big and small, and revisit them on "On this day"
📖 Albums — every kid and pet gets an automatic photo album, laid out in real collage pages with chapter dividers, scroll or page-turn through it, and export the whole thing as a PDF
🔒 Private by default — only the family members you invite can ever see your journal
🌐 5 languages — English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese, and Spanish

PRICING
Billed once per family — invite everyone else in with a family code, free.
• Free — 1GB storage, up to 1 kid/pet, occasional interstitial ads, 30-day free Pro trial
• Pro ($3.99/month) — 5GB storage (+5GB add-on available on either plan), unlimited kids & pets, no ads

Built by a parent, for parents — thanks for trying it.
```

## Data safety section (Play) / Privacy nutrition label (App Store) — data types to declare

- **Email address** — collected via Google Sign-In, used for account identification, not shared with third parties
- **Photos, videos, audio (voice memos)** — user-uploaded content, stored to run the core journaling feature, not shared with third parties, deletable by the user
- **User-generated text** — journal entries, not shared with third parties
- **Purchase history** — Stripe subscription/add-on status, used for billing only
- Encrypted in transit; family-scoped access control enforced server-side (see `requireSession()` pattern throughout the codebase)
- No advertising ID sharing beyond what AdMob's SDK itself requires for rewarded/interstitial ads (disclose AdMob as a third-party SDK)
