# Sprout promo video — script + shot list (draft)

For App Store preview, Play Store promo video, and the sprout.sl-studio.dev homepage hero video. English-first, but the "5 languages" beat is a required story point in every cut.

All footage described here uses **fake sample content** — a fictional family, fictional kid — not real user data. See the "Sample content to create" section at the bottom for exactly what to seed in a demo account before filming/recording.

---

## The core pitch (used to derive every cut below)

**The problem:** Parents already run an informal "family SNS" — a KakaoTalk family room, a group text, a shared photo album — to keep grandparents and family updated on the kids. It works, but it's messy: updates get buried under other chatter, nothing is organized by date or by kid, nobody can search "when did she start walking," and posting to Instagram means strangers and an algorithm see your kid too.

**The pitch:** Sprout is the private family group chat, redesigned to actually be a journal. Same instinct — share the moment with the people who love this kid — but organized, permanent, and yours alone. No public feed, no strangers, no algorithm.

**Why it "works" as a concept (say this explicitly in the longer cuts):**
1. It doesn't ask families to adopt a new habit — they're already sharing kid updates in a group chat. Sprout just gives that habit a proper home.
2. Multi-language support removes the friction that kills most "let's use an app for this" attempts in mixed-language / diaspora families — **important scope note:** this is UI-chrome translation only (menus, buttons, screen labels render in the reader's language). It does **not** translate what anyone actually writes — a journal entry written in English stays in English no matter who's reading it or what language their app is set to. Every script beat below must reflect this exactly; do not imply content gets translated.
3. Privacy is structural, not a settings toggle — there is no public account type to accidentally end up on.

## Cut B — App Store preview (Apple, ≤30s, portrait, actual on-device capture required)

Apple's guidelines require app previews to be **actual screen recordings of the app in use** — no separate lifestyle B-roll, no actors. Text overlays and music are fine; a voiceover is optional and less common for App Store previews (many top-charting apps skip VO entirely and rely on captions). Written here **with captions only, no VO**, since that's the safer default for Apple review.

| Time | Screen recording | Caption overlay |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Journal home (calendar + entry form) | "Your family's private journal" |
| 0:05–0:10 | Feed — "First bath" entry, real photo | "Every moment, saved" |
| 0:10–0:15 | Feed — Tummy-time entry with milestone badge | "Every first, tracked" |
| 0:15–0:20 | Milestones grid | "All your milestones, in one place" |
| 0:20–0:28 | Settings, Language section — shown in English (all 5 language options visible, not switched live) | "5 languages. Private by default." |

**Note (2026-08-11 revision):** this replaces the earlier draft, which put a live English→한국어 language switch at 0:19–0:25. Per direction, the actual delivered cut (`sprout-demo-cut.mp4`) keeps Settings in its English state throughout — it shows the 5-language picker as proof of support without dramatizing a live switch. The "Language-support beat" section below (originally written as non-negotiable for every cut) is superseded for this cut; update it if Cuts A/C still want the live-switch treatment.

## Cut C — Play Store promo video (YouTube link, 30–45s recommended)

Google is more permissive than Apple about mixing screen capture with other footage, but a tight screen-capture-driven cut converts better than an over-produced one for a utility app like this. Recommend reusing **Cut B's shot list almost exactly**, extended slightly:

- Use Cut B's 6 beats (0:00–0:30) unchanged.
- Add one more beat at the end (0:30–0:40): the family invite-code screen ("Invite your partner, grandparents, or anyone else") followed by the Play Store "Install" call-to-action card.
- VO is optional here too, but Play Store audiences skew slightly more tolerant of a short VO — if adding one, reuse Cut A's VO lines for the matching beats rather than writing new copy.

## Language-support beat — non-negotiable in every cut

Every cut (A/B/C) must show, not just claim, the language switch:
1. Open Settings.
2. Tap the language picker.
3. Visibly select at least 2 non-English languages in sequence (recommend 한국어 then one more — 日本語 or 中文 — since Korean is the most legible at a glance for the target early-adopter audience described below).
4. Let the UI chrome (nav labels, buttons) visibly re-render in the new language before cutting away — a static badge/text overlay claiming "5 languages" without showing it happen reads as unverified marketing copy, not proof.
